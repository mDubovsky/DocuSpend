# DocuSpend

Turn a bank statement into a clear picture of where your money went.

Give DocuSpend a statement (PDF, spreadsheet, CSV, …) and get charts showing when and how you spent: how much went to food this month, how much to travel, and so on.

**Status:** idea stage. Nothing is built yet; we are defining the product first.
